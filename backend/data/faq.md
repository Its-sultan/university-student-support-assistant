# University Student Support FAQ

This document is used by the RAG retriever to ground the assistant's answers.
Edit these sections to match your own university's real policies.

## Course Registration
Course registration opens at the start of each semester through the Student Information System (SIS) portal.
Students must clear any outstanding fees before registering. Registration closes two weeks after the
semester begins. Late registration is allowed within the third week but attracts a late-registration fee.
To register: log in to the SIS portal, select "Course Registration", choose your courses for the semester,
and submit for your academic advisor's approval. Contact the Registrar's Office for registration problems.

## Examination Rules
Students must carry their student ID card to every examination. Arriving more than 30 minutes late is not
permitted, and no student may leave during the first 30 minutes. Mobile phones and unauthorized materials
are strictly prohibited in the examination hall. A minimum of 75% class attendance is required to be
eligible to sit final examinations. Examination misconduct is treated as a serious disciplinary offence.
Supplementary examinations are available for students who fail a course, subject to faculty approval.

## Library Services
The main library is open Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM, and weekends from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
Undergraduate students may borrow up to 5 books for 14 days; postgraduate students up to 10 books for 30 days.
Overdue books attract a daily fine. The library provides e-books, journal databases, study rooms, and printing
services. Use your student ID to access the library and the online catalogue. Lost books must be replaced or
paid for at current value.

## ICT Support
The ICT Help Desk supports student email, Wi-Fi, the learning management system (LMS), and the student portal.
For password resets, use the self-service reset link or visit the ICT office with your student ID.
Campus Wi-Fi is available in all academic buildings and hostels; connect using your student credentials.
Report technical issues by emailing the ICT Help Desk or submitting a ticket through the support portal.
The ICT office is open Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

## Hostel Application
Hostel (accommodation) applications are submitted online through the Accommodation portal before each academic year.
Allocation is based on availability, distance from campus, and special needs, on a first-come first-served basis.
Successful applicants must pay the accommodation fee to confirm their room. Hostel residents must follow the
accommodation rules, including visiting hours and noise regulations. Contact the Dean of Students Office for
accommodation enquiries and complaints.

## Fee Payment
Tuition and other fees are paid through the university's official bank accounts or the online payment gateway.
Always use your registration number as the payment reference. Fees should be paid before the registration
deadline each semester. Proof of payment must be uploaded to the student portal. Students facing financial
difficulty may apply for a payment plan or scholarship through the Finance Office. Keep all payment receipts.

## Academic Calendar
The academic year is divided into two semesters, each about 15 weeks long, followed by an examination period.
The calendar includes registration weeks, teaching weeks, mid-semester breaks, examination periods, and
result-release dates. The official academic calendar is published on the university website and the student
portal at the start of each academic year. Always check the calendar for important deadlines.

## Student Conduct
All students must abide by the university's code of conduct. Prohibited behaviour includes examination
misconduct, plagiarism, harassment, vandalism, and substance abuse. Disciplinary cases are handled by the
Students' Disciplinary Committee, which may issue warnings, suspension, or expulsion depending on severity.
Students have the right to a fair hearing. Respect for staff, fellow students, and university property is
expected at all times.

## Contact Information

- Finance Office: fees, payments, and scholarships
- ICT Help Desk: email, Wi-Fi, portal, and LMS support
- Library: borrowing, databases, and study spaces
- Dean of Students Office: hostels, welfare, and conduct
For anything not covered here, contact the relevant office during working hours (Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM).
